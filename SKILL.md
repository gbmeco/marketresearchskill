# Market Research Skill

Use this workflow to produce concise, source-aware market research briefs.

## Workflow

1. Identify the target company, product, market, and user segment.
2. Gather public information from the company website, docs, pricing pages, blog, job posts, and reputable third-party sources.
3. Summarize the company profile: business model, customers, products, positioning, and recent signals.
4. Identify potential pain points, buying triggers, competitors, and outreach angles.
5. Produce a structured brief with clear uncertainty notes.

## Output Format

- Company overview
- Products and positioning
- Target customers
- Market signals
- Competitors
- Possible needs or pain points
- Lead qualification notes
- Suggested outreach angle
- Sources checked
- Confidence level
